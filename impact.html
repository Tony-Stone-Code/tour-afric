Conservation in Action

Mole Anti-Poaching Patrols

Supporting 24/7 ranger patrols to protect elephants, antelopes, and other wildlife from poaching. Tourism revenue directly funds equipment, training, and salaries for 45 rangers at Ghana's largest wildlife reserve.

2023 Impact: Zero elephant poaching incidents, 89% reduction in illegal hunting.

Kakum Community Guide Program

Training and employing guides from nearby villages to lead canopy walk tours. All guides receive ecological training, first aid certification, and fair compensation that exceeds regional standards by 40%.

2023 Impact: 62 guides trained; $180K in guide wages directly to families.

Ada Mangrove Restoration

Replanting critical mangrove forests at the Volta estuary to protect coastline, provide fish nursery habitats, and sequester carbon. Community members earn income as restoration workers and mangrove monitors.

2023 Impact: 15,000 mangrove seedlings planted; 8 hectares restored.

Transparent Operations

We believe in full transparency about where your tourism dollars go. Here's how we split revenue from every tour:

Local Guides & Communities 45%
Conservation & Wildlife Protection 25%
Operations & Logistics 20%
Sustainable Growth & Reinvestment 10%

This model ensures 70% of every dollar directly benefits conservation and Ghanaian communities.
