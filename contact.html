Quick Answers

Do I need a visa to visit Ghana?

Most visitors need a visa. Ghana offers e-visas for citizens of many countries, which can be applied for online before travel. We provide detailed visa guidance once you book.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, credit cards (Visa, Mastercard), and PayPal. A 30% deposit secures your booking, with the balance due 30 days before departure.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancellations 60+ days before departure: full refund minus 10% processing fee. 30-59 days: 50% refund. Less than 30 days: no refund, but we can reschedule for a future date.

Are tours suitable for families with children?

Absolutely! Many of our tours are family-friendly. We offer modified itineraries for families with young children, including shorter hikes and more cultural activities.
