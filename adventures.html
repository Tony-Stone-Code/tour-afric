Frequently Asked Questions

What should I pack for adventure tours?

Essential items include comfortable hiking boots, lightweight breathable clothing, sun protection (hat, sunscreen), insect repellent, reusable water bottle, and a camera. We provide detailed packing lists for each tour.

What's the best season for adventures?

Ghana's dry season (November-March) offers excellent conditions for most adventures. The rainy season (April-October) brings lush landscapes and fewer crowds, with certain waterfalls at their most spectacular.

Are the tours safe?

Safety is our top priority. All guides are certified and experienced. Tours are designed with appropriate safety margins, and we maintain comprehensive insurance and emergency protocols.

How does tourism support sustainability?

Tour fees directly fund conservation projects, anti-poaching efforts, and community development. Local guides receive fair wages, and we offset carbon emissions for all transportation.
