Ghana's coastline blends golden beaches, historic castles, vibrant fishing communities, and rich marine life. Experience responsible coastal tourism that protects turtle nesting sites, supports local fishermen, and preserves ocean ecosystems.

Featured Coastal Destinations

Central Region

Cape Coast & Elmina

Explore UNESCO World Heritage castles with deep historical significance. Beautiful beaches meet poignant heritage sites. Visit local fishing communities and enjoy fresh seafood.

  • Cape Coast Castle guided tours
  • Elmina Castle and St. George's Castle
  • Pristine beaches for swimming
  • Local fishing village experiences

Greater Accra

Ada Foah Estuary

Where the Volta River meets the Atlantic. Explore mangrove forests, spot diverse birdlife, and relax on quiet sandy beaches away from the crowds.

  • Boat tours through mangrove channels
  • Bird watching paradise (250+ species)
  • Traditional Dangme cultural experiences
  • Beach relaxation and water sports

Western Region

Busua & Ahanta Surf Culture

Ghana's premier surf destination with consistent waves and laid-back beach towns. Learn to surf, enjoy fresh coconuts, and immerse in coastal Ahanta culture.

  • Surf lessons for all skill levels
  • Beach bonfires and reggae vibes
  • Nearby Dixcove Fort exploration
  • Ahanta cultural festivals and traditions
View Details
Western Region

Nzulezu Stilt Village

A unique floating village built entirely on stilts over Lake Tadane. Canoe through raffia palm forests to reach this 500-year-old community.

  • Guided canoe journey through wetlands
  • UNESCO World Heritage tentative list
  • Experience life on water
  • Support sustainable village tourism

Greater Accra

Kokrobite Beach Retreat

Just 30km from Accra, this bohemian beach town offers golden sands, drumming circles, and vibrant nightlife combined with peaceful daytime relaxation.

  • Learn traditional Ga drumming and dancing
  • Horseback riding on the beach
  • Art galleries and craft markets
  • Easy access from Accra

Our Marine Conservation Commitment

Turtle Protection

Supporting nesting site monitoring and protection programs along Ghana's coast. All tours respect turtle conservation zones.

Responsible Diving

Reef-safe practices, no-touch policies, and education about marine ecosystems. Partner with certified eco-diving operators.

Beach Clean-ups

Monthly community beach cleaning events. Travelers can participate in plastic reduction initiatives during their stay.

Every coastal tour contributes to marine habitat restoration and fishing community support programs.
